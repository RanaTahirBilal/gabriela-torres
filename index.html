About

Postsecondary success is
a question of mobility.

Gabriela Torres is Deputy Director, Transformation at Scale on the Postsecondary Success team at the Gates Foundation. She leads the team in working with partners across the country to understand, implement, learn from and share evidence-based transformation that leads to success at scale.

The strategy she is responsible for starts from a plain premise: postsecondary success is crucial to economic and social mobility, and it is not acceptable that some students, especially Black, Indigenous, Latinx and low-income students, face specific and systemic barriers that prevent them from realising the full value of postsecondary education.

Transformation at Scale

Leading a team whose remit is not a single campus but change that holds across many institutions at once.

Evidence, then practice

Understand, implement, learn from and share. The sequence matters as much as the outcome.

Student affairs at heart

Learnings from student-facing roles still guide the leadership she brings to national strategy.

Focus

What the strategy is actually for.

Economic and social mobility

Postsecondary education treated as the mechanism it is, not as an end in itself.

Systemic barriers

Named plainly: barriers that fall specifically on Black, Indigenous, Latinx and low-income students.

Evidence-based transformation

Change that can be measured, repeated, and handed to the next institution.

Partners across the country

The work is done with institutions and networks, not delivered to them.
